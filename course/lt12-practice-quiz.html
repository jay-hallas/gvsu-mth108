    Worksheet Learning Target 12: Linear Models in Context
    

    Objectives

    • Given a linear word situation, I can write an equation; identify and explain the meaning of the slope and the vertical intercept in context; find \(f(a)\) and explain its meaning; find \(x\) given \(f(a)\) and explain its meaning; and determine what inputs make sense.
      🔗
    🔗

    1. Taking a Rideshare.

    A rideshare app charges a \(\$3.50\) booking fee for every ride, plus \(\$1.75\) for each mile driven.
    🔗

    (a)

    Write an equation for the total cost of a ride in terms of the number of miles driven. Be sure to define each variable, including units.
    🔗
    Answer.
    Let \(m\) be the number of miles driven and let \(C\) be the total cost of the ride in dollars. Then \(C = 1.75m + 3.5\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    How much does an \(8\)-mile ride cost? Explain what your answer means in this situation.
    🔗
    Answer.
    Substitute \(m = 8\text{:}\) \(C = 1.75(8) + 3.5 = 14 + 3.5 = 17.5\text{.}\) An \(8\)-mile ride costs \(\$17.50\text{,}\) including the booking fee.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (c)

    A ride cost \(\$24.50\text{.}\) How many miles long was the ride? Explain what your answer means in this situation.
    🔗
    Answer.
    Set \(C = 24.5\) and solve for \(m\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 24.5 \amp= 1.75m + 3.5\\ 21 \amp= 1.75m\\ 12 \amp= m \end{align*}
    🔗
    A ride that costs \(\$24.50\) covered \(12\) miles.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    2. Dining Dollars.

    A student starts the semester with \(\$240\) in dining dollars on their campus ID card. They spend \(\$8\) each day on lunch.
    🔗

    (a)

    Write an equation for the balance on the card in terms of the number of days since the semester started. Be sure to define each variable, including units.
    🔗
    Answer.
    Let \(d\) be the number of days since the semester started and let \(B\) be the balance on the card in dollars. Then \(B = 240 - 8d\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    What is the balance after \(15\) days? Explain what your answer means in this situation.
    🔗
    Answer.
    Substitute \(d = 15\text{:}\) \(B = 240 - 8(15) = 240 - 120 = 120\text{.}\) After \(15\) days of buying lunch, the student has \(\$120\) left on the card, which is half of what they started with.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (c)

    After how many days will the student have \(\$56\) left? Explain what your answer means in this situation.
    🔗
    Answer.
    Set \(B = 56\) and solve for \(d\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 56 \amp= 240 - 8d\\ -184 \amp= -8d\\ 23 \amp= d \end{align*}
    🔗
    After \(23\) days of buying lunch, the student has \(\$56\) in dining dollars left.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    3. A Gym Membership.

    A gym charges a one-time sign-up fee plus the same fee every month. After \(3\) months, a member has paid a total of \(\$95\text{.}\) After \(7\) months, they have paid a total of \(\$175\text{.}\)
    🔗

    (a)

    Find the rate of change, including units. What does it tell you about the cost of the membership?
    🔗
    Answer.
    \begin{gather*} \frac{175 - 95}{7 - 3} = \frac{80}{4} = 20 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    The rate of change is \(\$20\) per month. The gym charges \(\$20\) each month, so the total paid goes up by \(\$20\) for each additional month of membership.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    What is the initial value? What does it mean in this situation?
    🔗
    Answer.
    Work back \(3\) months from \(\$95\text{:}\) \(95 - 3(20) = 95 - 60 = 35\text{.}\) The initial value is \(\$35\text{.}\) This is the one-time sign-up fee, the amount paid at \(0\) months before any monthly fees.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (c)

    Write an equation for the total amount paid in terms of the number of months. Be sure to define each variable, including units.
    🔗
    Answer.
    Let \(n\) be the number of months of membership and let \(T\) be the total amount paid in dollars. Then \(T = 20n + 35\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (d)

    How much will a member have paid in total after one year? Explain what your answer means in this situation.
    🔗
    Answer.
    One year is \(12\) months. Substitute \(n = 12\text{:}\) \(T = 20(12) + 35 = 240 + 35 = 275\text{.}\) After one year, a member has paid \(\$275\) in total, including the sign-up fee.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    4. An Electric Scooter.

    A student rides an electric scooter to campus, and the battery drains at a steady rate as they ride. After riding \(5\) miles, the battery shows \(80\%\) charge. After riding \(12\) miles, it shows \(52\%\) charge.
    🔗

    (a)

    Find the rate of change, including units. What does it tell you about the battery?
    🔗
    Answer.
    \begin{gather*} \frac{52 - 80}{12 - 5} = \frac{-28}{7} = -4 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    The rate of change is \(-4\) percent per mile. The battery loses \(4\%\) of its charge for each additional mile ridden.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    What is the initial value? What does it mean in this situation?
    🔗
    Answer.
    Work back \(5\) miles from \(80\%\text{.}\) Each mile used \(4\%\text{,}\) so add it back: \(80 + 5(4) = 80 + 20 = 100\text{.}\) The initial value is \(100\%\text{,}\) which means the battery was fully charged when the student started riding.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (c)

    Write an equation for the battery charge in terms of the number of miles ridden. Be sure to define each variable, including units.
    🔗
    Answer.
    Let \(x\) be the number of miles ridden and let \(P\) be the battery charge as a percent. Then \(P = 100 - 4x\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (d)

    What will the battery show after riding \(18\) miles? Explain what your answer means in this situation.
    🔗
    Answer.
    Substitute \(x = 18\text{:}\) \(P = 100 - 4(18) = 100 - 72 = 28\text{.}\) After riding \(18\) miles, the battery has \(28\%\) of its charge left.
