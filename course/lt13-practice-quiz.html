    Worksheet Learning Target 13: Solving Linear Equations
    

    Objectives

    • I can solve linear equations symbolically or with a graph.
      🔗
    🔗

    1. Problem 1.

    Solve the equation. Show your work and check your answer.
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 4x + 3 = x + 9 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Answer.
    Subtract \(x\) from both sides:
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 3x + 3 = 9 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Subtract \(3\) from both sides and divide by \(3\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 3x \amp= 6\\ x \amp= 2 \end{align*}
    🔗
    Check: \(4(2) + 3 = 11\) and \(2 + 9 = 11\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    2. Problem 2.

    Solve the equation. Show your work and check your answer.
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 5n - 7 = 2n + 8 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Answer.
    Subtract \(2n\) from both sides:
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 3n - 7 = 8 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Add \(7\) to both sides and divide by \(3\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 3n \amp= 15\\ n \amp= 5 \end{align*}
    🔗
    Check: \(5(5) - 7 = 18\) and \(2(5) + 8 = 18\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    3. Problem 3.

    Solve the equation. Show your work and check your answer.
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} -3(x + 4) = 2x - 16 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Answer.
    Distribute the \(-3\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} -3x - 12 = 2x - 16 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Subtract \(2x\) from both sides, then add \(12\) to both sides:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} -5x - 12 \amp= -16\\ -5x \amp= -4\\ x \amp= \frac{4}{5} \end{align*}
    🔗
    Check: \(-3\left(\frac{4}{5} + 4\right) = -3\left(\frac{24}{5}\right) = -\frac{72}{5}\) and \(2\left(\frac{4}{5}\right) - 16 = \frac{8}{5} - \frac{80}{5} = -\frac{72}{5}\text{.}\) A solution does not have to be a whole number.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    4. Problem 4.

    Solve the equation. Show your work and check your answer.
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 2(t - 5) + 3t = 20 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Answer.
    Distribute the \(2\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 2t - 10 + 3t = 20 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Combine the like terms on the left:
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 5t - 10 = 20 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Add \(10\) to both sides and divide by \(5\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 5t \amp= 30\\ t \amp= 6 \end{align*}
    🔗
    Check: \(2(6 - 5) + 3(6) = 2 + 18 = 20\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    5. Problem 5.

    Solve the equation. Show your work and check your answer.
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 7 - (y + 3) = 3y - 8 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Answer.
    Distribute the negative sign to both terms in the parentheses:
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 7 - y - 3 = 3y - 8 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Combine the like terms on the left:
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 4 - y = 3y - 8 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Add \(y\) to both sides, then add \(8\) to both sides:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 4 \amp= 4y - 8\\ 12 \amp= 4y\\ 3 \amp= y \end{align*}
    🔗
    Check: \(7 - (3 + 3) = 1\) and \(3(3) - 8 = 1\text{.}\) A common mistake is writing \(7 - y + 3\text{;}\) the negative sign applies to the \(3\) as well.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    6. Problem 6.

    Solve the equation. Show your work and check your answer.
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 4(2a - 1) = 3(a + 7) \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Answer.
    Distribute on both sides:
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 8a - 4 = 3a + 21 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Subtract \(3a\) from both sides, then add \(4\) to both sides:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 5a - 4 \amp= 21\\ 5a \amp= 25\\ a \amp= 5 \end{align*}
    🔗
    Check: \(4(2(5) - 1) = 4(9) = 36\) and \(3(5 + 7) = 3(12) = 36\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    7. Problem 7.

    Solve the equation. Show your work and check your answer.
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 6 - 2(w - 3) = 5(w + 1) - 4w + 1 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Answer.
    Distribute on both sides:
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 6 - 2w + 6 = 5w + 5 - 4w + 1 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Combine the like terms on each side:
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 12 - 2w = w + 6 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Add \(2w\) to both sides, then subtract \(6\) from both sides:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 12 \amp= 3w + 6\\ 6 \amp= 3w\\ 2 \amp= w \end{align*}
    🔗
    Check: \(6 - 2(2 - 3) = 6 + 2 = 8\) and \(5(2 + 1) - 4(2) + 1 = 15 - 8 + 1 = 8\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    8. Problem 8.

    Solve the equation. Show your work and check your answer.
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} \frac{1}{2}(6k + 4) - k = 3(k - 2) + 4 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Answer.
    Distribute on both sides:
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 3k + 2 - k = 3k - 6 + 4 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Combine the like terms on each side:
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 2k + 2 = 3k - 2 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Subtract \(2k\) from both sides, then add \(2\) to both sides:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 2 \amp= k - 2\\ 4 \amp= k \end{align*}
    🔗
    Check: \(\frac{1}{2}(28) - 4 = 14 - 4 = 10\) and \(3(4 - 2) + 4 = 6 + 4 = 10\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    9. Problem 9 (Challenge).

    This equation has squared terms, but it can still be solved like a linear equation. Solve it, show your work, and check your answer.
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} m(m + 3) - 4 = m^2 - 2m + 11 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Answer.
    Distribute the \(m\) on the left:
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} m^2 + 3m - 4 = m^2 - 2m + 11 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Subtract \(m^2\) from both sides. The squared terms cancel, leaving a linear equation:
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 3m - 4 = -2m + 11 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Add \(2m\) to both sides, then add \(4\) to both sides:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 5m - 4 \amp= 11\\ 5m \amp= 15\\ m \amp= 3 \end{align*}
    🔗
    Check: \(3(3 + 3) - 4 = 18 - 4 = 14\) and \(3^2 - 2(3) + 11 = 9 - 6 + 11 = 14\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    10. Problem 10 (Challenge).

    Treat \(z^3\) as a single quantity and solve the equation the same way you would a linear equation. Then find \(z\text{.}\) Show your work and check your answer.
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 5z^3 + 4 = 2z^3 - 20 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Answer.
    Subtract \(2z^3\) from both sides, then subtract \(4\) from both sides:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 3z^3 + 4 \amp= -20\\ 3z^3 \amp= -24\\ z^3 \amp= -8 \end{align*}
    🔗
    The number whose cube is \(-8\) is \(-2\text{,}\) since \((-2)(-2)(-2) = -8\text{.}\) So \(z = -2\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Check: \(5(-8) + 4 = -36\) and \(2(-8) - 20 = -36\text{.}\)
    🔗
